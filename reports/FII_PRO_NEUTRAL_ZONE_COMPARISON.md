# FII VIEW x PRO VIEW — Neutral Zone Threshold Comparison

**Reports compared**: ±10K, ±20K, ±25K, ±30K neutral zones
**Dataset**: 1,488 days | 2020-08-06 to 2026-09-09

This report compares all four neutral zone threshold analyses side by side to reveal which signals are robust across definitions and which are artifacts of a specific threshold.

### Label Guide
- **NeutralX** = composite between ±XK (e.g. Neutral10 = ±10K, Neutral30 = ±30K)
- **MildBullX** = Mildly Bullish = composite +XK to +50K
- **MildBearX** = Mildly Bearish = composite -XK to -50K
- **Bullish / Bearish** = composite ±50K to ±100K (fixed across all thresholds)
- **Strong Bullish / Strong Bearish** = composite beyond ±100K (fixed)

**Note**: In sections 2-6 where combos are compared across thresholds, "Neutral", "MildBull", and "MildBear" in each column refer to that column's threshold definition (e.g. under ±10K column, "Neutral" means Neutral10 = ±10K range).

## 7. Key Findings

### Signals you CAN trust (threshold-proof):

1. **Bearish FII + Bullish PRO = 83% Green** — The strongest contrarian signal. Identical across all 4 thresholds. When FII sells but PRO buys, the market recovers.

2. **Bullish FII + NeutralX PRO = 71-78% Green** — Consistent across all thresholds with growing sample size (9d at ±10K → 21d at ±30K). The tighter the neutral, the stronger the signal but the smaller the sample.

3. **Bullish FII + Strong Bullish PRO = 40% Green, 134 days** — The biggest bearish trap. Completely threshold-immune. When both are aggressively long, the market fades.

4. **Strong Bearish + Strong Bearish = 42% Green, 90 days** — Double bearish alignment does NOT mean the market drops more. It's already a coin flip heading slightly bearish.

5. **"Down to Up" remains the dominant winning pattern** — Across all thresholds, every combination above 55% Green shows morning weakness followed by afternoon recovery.

### Signals you should NOT trust (threshold-dependent):

1. **"Mild" agreement patterns** (MildBullX+MildBullX, MildBearX+MildBearX) — These are the most sensitive to threshold choice. Their Green% swings 30-37% across thresholds. At wider thresholds (±25K, ±30K) they look excellent but have tiny samples; at tighter thresholds they dilute into mediocrity.

2. **NeutralX + Bearish** — Ranges from 59% (Neutral30, 88 days) to 78% (Neutral10, 27 days). The signal strengthens as neutral narrows, but the sample also shrinks. Use with caution.

3. **Any combo involving MildBearX PRO** — Results vary wildly: Bullish+MildBear10 = 57% vs Bullish+MildBear30 = 33%. The classification boundary matters more than the signal.

### Optimal Threshold Recommendation:

- **For signal reliability**: Use ±20K or ±25K. These balance sample size with signal strength.
- **For conservative trading**: Only trust the 8 "threshold-proof good" combos from Section 4.
- **For maximum caution**: Avoid any combo that appears in Section 6 (threshold-sensitive) unless you have additional confirmation.
